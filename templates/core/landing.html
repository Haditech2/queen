How it stays safe

Every message goes through a Safety Pipeline.

Your message is checked before the AI sees it, and the reply is checked again before you do.

  1. You write

    Your message is validated and stored only in your private history.

  2. Crisis check

    Risk is rated low to imminent. High risk skips the AI and shows emergency contacts.

  3. Your context

    Language, tone and campus details are added so the reply fits you.

  4. AI reply

    Gemini, or another configured model, drafts a short, warm response.

  5. Reply review

    Anything that diagnoses, prescribes or stereotypes is blocked and replaced.

Our boundaries

A good listener, not a doctor.

MindBridge will never tell you that you have a condition, never suggest medication, and never pretend to replace a counsellor or clinician. When you need one, it points you to the NSUK Health Centre, the Guidance & Counselling Unit or FMC Keffi.
