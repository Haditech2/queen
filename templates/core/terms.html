Terms of service

Effective: September 2026

Essential clarification: MindBridge is an informational, emotional support, and self-reflection web platform. It is NOT an emergency service, hospital, medical clinic, psychiatrist, or licensed therapist.

1. Scope of service

MindBridge offers culturally attuned first-line support, evidence-informed coping exercises (e.g. breathwork, grounding), educational materials, and a directory of verified Nigerian mental health centers.

2. Medical & emergency non-liability

If you are in acute crisis, experiencing severe medical symptoms, or having thoughts of self-harm or suicide, you must immediately contact local emergency services (112), call the MANI crisis line at 0809 111 6264, or visit the nearest hospital emergency department.

3. Responsible use

Users agree not to attempt to bypass safety layers, generate harmful materials, or disrupt the availability of the platform for others.

4. Your account

You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential. You can change your password, export your data, or delete your account at any time from Privacy & Settings.
