Immediate safety steps during a crisis

1 · Put time between thought and action

Intense emotional storms peak and subside. Promise yourself you will wait and take zero irreversible actions for the next 2 hours while seeking support.

2 · Tell a trusted person or helpline

Call a hotline above or text a trusted friend, family member, mentor, or neighbor. Tell them: "I am going through a really intense crisis right now and need you to stay with me."

3 · Make your environment safe

Step away from any lethal items, medications, or high places. Move to a public room, a living room with family, or visit a hospital emergency department.
